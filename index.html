Beyond the portfolio

A life in motion.

I’m curious about how a place shapes the way we see the world. In 2024, City Models invited me to Paris, where I spent a month immersed in the city’s rhythm. Since then, travelling through Norway, Italy, Sweden and Germany has deepened my interest in new cultures, local food and everyday style. I work as a designer, make time for sport, and bring that visual curiosity to everything I do. Fashion brings these interests together—and it’s the industry I’m excited to grow in.
